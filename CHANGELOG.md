# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-28

- Initial release.
- Adds the Privatus Analytics tracking script to the head of every page, with no template changes.
- Settings page under Admin-Tools for the site ID and for each tracker module (engage, auto, vitals, clicks, ask).
- Your own visits are not counted while you are logged in, unless you turn that off.
- English and German.
